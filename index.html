Our Happy Place

Made by hand, from scratch, every single day

Sitio Café started with a simple idea: bring people together over really good coffee and food that actually tastes homemade, because it is. Everything on our counter is made in-house each morning, from our slow-roasted granola to the bread under your sandwich.

We're a small team who genuinely love what we do, and it shows in the little details: the perfectly pulled shot, the extra minute to say good morning, the table that's always kept for the regulars.
